VPN Fast Ultra — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026 · Türkçe

This policy explains which data the VPN Fast Ultra app (Google Play package com.vpnfastultra.app) processes, how long it is kept and who it is shared with. We tried to describe what actually happens; if any statement contradicts how the app behaves, please write to us. The Turkish version is the original; this is a translation.

Summary: The app requires no account and does not collect your name, email or phone number. The websites you visit and the content of your tunnel traffic are not logged. What we keep is the technical minimum needed to set up a tunnel to your device, for short periods (see 4). Your data is not sold and is not shared with third parties other than the ad network in the free tier and the infrastructure providers listed below. There are no ads with a subscription; once you subscribe, the ad system is not started at all.

1. Data controller

The app is developed and its servers are operated by Özdemir Bilişim ve Teknoloji Hizmetleri (Google Play developer name). Contact and all data requests: vpnfastultra@gmail.com

2. Data we process

2.1 An anonymous ID instead of an account

When you first open the app, an anonymous ID is created in the background: a randomly generated number (UUID). Nothing is asked of you; no name, email or phone number is collected. This number does not identify you on its own, but the technical records below (subscription token, device record) are kept under it.

2.2 Technical data kept to connect (device record)

One device record is kept per anonymous ID. Your device's private WireGuard key is generated on your device, stored encrypted on your device and is never sent to us.

2.3 IP address

  • VPN server: while you are connected, WireGuard keeps your device's current real IP address and port in memory so it can send packets back to you. It is not written to disk, does not appear in any log and is lost when the server restarts.
  • Account infrastructure (Supabase): when the app signs in, Supabase writes the request's IP address and browser information to its session record. We automatically delete these fields every 10 minutes. Supabase's infrastructure (gateway) logs also keep request IP addresses for a short operational period.
  • We do not use your IP address to locate you, build a profile or for any other purpose.

2.4 What we do not collect

  • Websites you visit, apps you use, the content of tunnel traffic
  • Connection history (when you connected and to where)
  • Location; contacts, photos, files or other content on your device

The VPN server keeps transferred byte counts only in memory (for the speed limit and the MB display in the app); these counters are not written to disk. The server's operational log contains only totals such as device counts; no device key or IP address is written, and the log is deleted after 7 days.

2.5 DNS

While the tunnel is on, your domain name lookups (DNS) go through the tunnel to Cloudflare's 1.1.1.1 resolver. We do not log the queries; Cloudflare processes them under its 1.1.1.1 privacy commitments. Cloudflare sees the VPN server's address, not your real IP address.

2.6 Ads (free use only)

In free use, ads are shown through Google AdMob. Google processes data such as your device's advertising ID under its own policy to select and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, consent for ads is requested with Google's consent form and can be changed at any time in Settings > Ad privacy options. See Google's privacy policy for how Google uses data. For subscribers the ad system is not started at all.

Rewarded ads: by watching an ad to the end you get full speed for 30 minutes (stacking up to 2 hours). To verify the reward, your anonymous ID is passed to Google when the ad loads; after the ad is watched, Google sends a signed notification to our server. We keep only the transaction number and the anonymous ID for 30 days, to prevent the same ad from being rewarded twice; the end time of full speed is kept with your device record. We do not keep which ad you watched.

2.7 Subscription

Payment is made through Google Play; your card details never reach us. When a purchase completes, the purchase token issued by Google Play is sent to our server and verified with the Google Play Developer API. We keep: the token, the plan (yearly / monthly / weekly), the subscription status, and the start and end dates. The subscription status is re-checked with Google Play once a day to pick up refunds, cancellations and plan changes. Your payment method, billing address or Google account name never reach us.

Premium is valid on one device at a time. When you tap Restore purchase on a new device, the subscription moves to that device and the previous device returns to free use.

4. Retention

  • Device record: deleted automatically after 14 days without any connection. A new record is created when you connect again.
  • IP address and browser information in the session record: deleted within 10 minutes.
  • Rewarded ad transaction numbers: 30 days.
  • Anonymous ID and all records linked to it: deleted automatically after 180 days of no use (if there is no valid subscription).
  • Subscription record: as long as the anonymous ID exists; deleted together with it.
  • Server operational log: 7 days.
  • If you uninstall the app, the key and session data on your device are deleted too. When you send a deletion request, your records are deleted within 30 days at the latest.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR and KVKK (Turkish Law No. 6698, Art. 11) you have the right to learn whether your data is processed, to request information and a copy (portability), rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, to object to processing, to learn who it was transferred to, and to claim compensation if you suffer damage. You can withdraw ad consent at any time in Settings.

Send your request to vpnfastultra@gmail.com. Because there are no accounts, please include the device ID shown on the My Subscription screen in the app (tap it to copy the full ID) so we can find your record. Requests are answered within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority or to the data protection authority of your country.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
