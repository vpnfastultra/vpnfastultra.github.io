VPN Fast Ultra — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026 · Version 2.1 · Türkçe

This policy explains which personal data the VPN Fast Ultra Android app (Google Play package com.vpnfastultra.app) processes, for what purposes and on what legal bases, who receives it, how long it is kept and how you can exercise your rights. It is our privacy notice under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Art. 13), the UK GDPR and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK, Art. 10). We have tried to describe what the app actually does; if you think any statement contradicts its behaviour, please write to us.

Summary: The app requires no account and does not ask for your name, email or phone number. We do not log the websites you visit, your DNS queries or the content of your tunnel traffic, and we keep no connection history. We keep the technical minimum needed to set up a tunnel, for short periods (see 7). Your data is not sold and is not shared with third parties other than the ad network in the free tier (Google AdMob) and the service providers listed below. Premium has no ads; once you subscribe, the ad system is not started at all.

This policy covers the app, our VPN servers, our back end and the website vpnfastultra.github.io. 3. Data we process

3.1 An anonymous ID instead of an account

After you accept the Privacy and Terms card shown on first launch, an anonymous ID is created in the background (the app makes no network request before you accept): a randomly generated number (UUID) and the session keys that belong to it. The time the ID was created and its last session time are also kept. Nothing is asked of you. The number does not reveal your name on its own, but because the technical records below are kept under it, we treat it as personal data covered by this policy. The "device ID" shown on the app's My Subscription screen is this number.

3.3 Your real IP address

  • VPN server: to send packets back to you, WireGuard keeps the real IP address and port your device last connected from in memory only. This may remain in memory after you disconnect, until your device record is removed from the server (see 7) or the server restarts; it is overwritten on a new connection. It is not written to disk, does not appear in operational logs and is not backed up.
  • Back end (Supabase): when the app signs in or refreshes its session, Supabase writes the request's IP address and client information (user agent) to its session record. We automatically delete these fields every 10 minutes. Supabase's platform logs (API gateway and function logs) also keep request IP addresses for their own retention period (1 to 7 days depending on the plan).
  • Latency check: for the latency (ms) shown on the location screen, the app sends a short HTTPS request to the server. These requests are not logged.
  • We do not use your IP address to locate you, build a profile or for any purpose not described here.

3.4 Traffic, connections and DNS

  • We do not log the websites you visit, the apps you use, the content of tunnel traffic or your connection history (when you connected and to where).
  • To enforce the speed limit and feed the data counter in the app, the VPN server keeps transferred byte counts in memory only; these counters are not written to disk.
  • The server's operational log contains only totals such as device counts; no device key or IP address is written. It is deleted after 7 days.
  • DNS: while the tunnel is on, your domain name lookups are answered by our own DNS resolver running on the VPN server you are connected to; they are not sent to a third-party DNS provider. Queries are not logged (query logging is off); the resolver only keeps domain answers in a short-lived cache.

3.5 Ads (free use only)

In free use, banner, interstitial, app-open and rewarded ads are shown through Google AdMob. The ad system does not start before you accept the Privacy and Terms notice shown on first launch, and never starts for Premium subscribers. When ads are shown, Google may collect and process, under its own policies, data such as your device's advertising ID and app set ID, your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information, your interactions with ads (impressions, clicks, views) and diagnostic (performance, crash) data. Google uses this data to serve and measure ads, to prevent abuse and fraud and — where you have consented or the applicable law allows — to personalise ads. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: consent for ads and personalisation is requested with Google's consent form. You can change your choice or withdraw consent at any time in Settings > Ad privacy options.
  • All countries: you can delete or reset your advertising ID in your Android device's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads); personalised ads are not shown with a deleted advertising ID.
  • If you do not want to see ads, you can switch to Premium.

3.6 Rewarded ads

If you watch a rewarded ad to the end, you get 30 minutes of full speed (stacking up to 2 hours). To verify that the reward matches an ad that was actually watched, your anonymous ID is passed to Google when the ad loads; after the ad is watched, Google sends a signed notification to our server. To prevent the same ad from being rewarded twice, we keep only Google's transaction number and your anonymous ID for 30 days. The end time of full speed is kept with your device record. We do not keep which ad you watched.

3.7 Subscription and payments

Premium is bought through Google Play; your payment details (card, billing address, Google account name and email) never reach us. When a purchase completes, the purchase token issued by Google Play is sent to our server and verified with the Google Play Developer API. We keep: the token, the plan (yearly / monthly / weekly), the subscription status, and the start and end dates. The status is re-checked with Google Play once a day to reflect refunds, cancellations and plan changes.

Premium is valid on one device at a time. When you tap Restore purchase on another device, the subscription record moves to that device's anonymous ID and the previous device returns to free use.

For sales, Google makes sales and payout reports available to us in Play Console, containing information such as order number, product, price, date and the buyer's country (in some countries state or postal code). We use these reports for our accounting and tax obligations; they are not matched with your anonymous ID.

3.8 Support and requests

When you email us, your email address, your name (if shown in the email), the content of your message and any other information you share (for example your device ID) are processed only to answer your request, find your record and, where necessary, prove that the request was handled. Our support mailbox runs on Google's Gmail service.

3.9 Data kept only on your device

Your WireGuard private key, session keys, language and app settings and the record of your first-launch acceptance are kept only on your device; secrets are kept in encrypted storage protected by the Android Keystore. App data is not included in Android cloud backups. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

3.10 Website

vpnfastultra.github.io is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages; it uses no cookies or analytics. The hosting provider GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes under its own privacy statement.

7. Retention and deletion

  • IP address and client information in the session record: deleted within 10 minutes.
  • Supabase platform logs: Supabase's retention period (1 to 7 days depending on the plan).
  • Real IP and port in server memory: until your device record is removed from the server or the server restarts; overwritten on a new connection.
  • Device record: deleted automatically after 14 days without any connection; a new record is created when you connect again. When you move to another device, the old record is deleted.
  • Rewarded-ad transaction records: 30 days.
  • Anonymous ID and all records linked to it (device record, reward end time, subscription record): deleted automatically after 180 days without any use if there is no valid subscription.
  • Server operational log: 7 days.
  • Google Play sales and payout reports: for the retention periods of Turkish tax and commercial law (5 years under tax law; up to 10 years for commercial books and records).
  • Support and request correspondence: 2 years after the request is closed; longer only while a dispute is pending.
  • Data on your device: until you uninstall the app.

Expired data is deleted by automated jobs. Deleted records may remain in the infrastructure provider's automatic backups, if any, until the backup expires (at most 7 days). When you ask for deletion, we delete your records within 30 days at the latest; data we must keep by law (e.g. accounting records) is deleted when that period ends.

8. Security

  • Traffic between your device and the VPN server is encrypted with the WireGuard protocol. All communication between the app and our back end uses HTTPS (TLS).
  • Your private key never leaves your device and is stored there in encrypted storage protected by the Android Keystore.
  • Row-level access rules apply in the database: the app can read only its own records, and subscription and device records can only be written by the server functions that verify them.
  • The VPN server accesses the database with a narrowly scoped key that can only read its own device list; the server's private key exists only on the server.
  • Access from the tunnel to the server's own services and to internal networks is blocked; the latency service and the HTTPS server in front of it do not log requests.

No system is entirely risk-free. If a breach affecting your data occurs, we will notify the competent authorities as required by law (under KVKK and the GDPR, within 72 hours) and inform you by the means available to us.

9.4 How to make a request

Because there are no accounts, we can only find your record by the anonymous ID. Please include the device ID shown on the app's My Subscription screen (tap it to copy the full ID) and send your request to vpnfastultra@gmail.com. Requests are answered free of charge within 30 days (for complex GDPR requests this may be extended by two months, in which case we will tell you). If we cannot verify that a request relates to your record, we may ask for more information.

Requests under KVKK must be made in Turkish in accordance with the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Application to the Data Controller (in writing to the address in section 1, by registered electronic mail (KEP), or by email signed with a secure electronic or mobile signature, including your name, national ID or passport number, address for notifications and the subject of the request). The Turkish version of this policy describes this procedure in detail.

9.5 Complaints

You can complain to the data protection authority where you live. Under KVKK, if your request is rejected, the answer is insufficient or no answer is given in time, you can complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board within 30 days of learning of the answer and in any case within 60 days of your request.

10. Limits of a VPN

A VPN encrypts your traffic between your device and our server and shows the sites you visit our server's address instead of your real IP address. It does not make you fully anonymous: accounts you sign in to, cookies, browser fingerprinting and apps' own data collection are not affected by the VPN. When the tunnel is off or the connection drops, your traffic may bypass the VPN; Settings > Kill switch takes you to the Android system setting that prevents this.

11. Legal requests and abuse

Because we do not log traffic content, visited addresses or connection history, we cannot hand over such information in response to any request. Since all users share the server's exit IP address, it is usually not possible for us to link a past connection made at a given time to a given address to a particular user. If we receive a lawful and binding request (e.g. a court order), we disclose only the minimal data we actually hold (see 3) and only to the extent legally required; where legally permitted, we will notify you of the request.

While investigating an ongoing attack, spam or similar abuse complaint, the server's current connection table may be observed live and the anonymous ID found to be involved may be blocked. This inspection is not recorded and is carried out only for that incident.

If the law requires us to keep additional data in the future, we will update this policy and inform you before we start doing so.

12. Children

The app is not directed at anyone under 18 and is offered on Google Play to users aged 18 and over. Because there are no accounts we do not verify age, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child is using the app, write to us and we will delete the related record.
